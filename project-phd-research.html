PhD Research · Tentative Direction · 2025–Present

Digital Heritage Engagement through XR, Gamification, and Human–AI Collaboration

This page outlines an evolving doctoral research direction. The final scope, title, and methodology will be refined through doctoral study.

Research Direction

The proposed research explores how extended reality, playful interaction, and human–AI collaboration can support public interpretation and meaningful engagement with built heritage. It provisionally focuses on participatory experience design, embodied interaction, and new forms of digital heritage communication.

Provisional Questions

  • How can XR move heritage audiences from passive viewing toward active interpretation?
  • How can gamification support sustained, meaningful engagement without trivialising heritage values?
  • How might human–AI collaboration enable plural and participatory heritage narratives?
