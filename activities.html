Human–AI Collaborative Interpretation with Generative Video

7–11 September 2026 · Lübeck, Germany

The HACI workshop explored how visitors can use spatially constrained image-to-video generation to animate activities depicted in Chinese classical paintings and engage with the relationships between tangible heritage spaces and intangible cultural practices.
