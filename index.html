Research Interests

My research investigates how Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), and Mixed Reality (MR) can enrich the interpretation and experience of architectural and cultural heritage. I am particularly interested in public engagement, gamified interaction, and digital technologies in heritage preservation and urban renewal.
